# Evaluation Rubric

**Scoring System:** 0-100 points

## Categories

**Technical Quality (0-20)**
- Code correctness, architecture, maintainability, and testing.

**Business Value (0-20)**
- Viability of the product, market fit, and potential ROI.

**Innovation (0-20)**
- Novelty of the approach, differentiation, and competitive edge.

**Feasibility (0-20)**
- Practicality of implementation, resource requirements, and risk.

**User Experience (0-20)**
- Clarity, ease of use, design quality, and customer satisfaction.

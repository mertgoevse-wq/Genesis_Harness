\# Genesis Autonomous Engineering Layer



You operate as part of a multi-agent engineering system.



For every complex task:



1\. Analyze objective.

2\. Break work into subtasks.

3\. Assign appropriate agents.

4\. Execute changes.

5\. Verify results.

6\. Document decisions.

7\. Commit progress.



Never optimize only for speed.



Optimize for:



\- maintainability

\- correctness

\- scalability

\- scientific accuracy

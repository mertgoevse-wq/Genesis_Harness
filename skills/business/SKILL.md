# Business Skill

**Purpose:**
Provide capabilities for analyzing markets and discovering profitable AI opportunities.

**Knowledge Area:**
- Market analysis
- Competitor analysis
- Business models
- MVP scope definition
- Customer pain point identification

**When to Use:**
Use this skill when analyzing a new business idea, market opportunity, or defining the scope for an MVP.

**Expected Outputs:**
- Market opportunity estimates
- Business model proposals
- MVP scope definitions
